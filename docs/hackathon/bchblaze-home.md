# Igniting BCH Innovation

BLAZE is an 8 day online hackathon (November 14th - 22nd 2026) for Bitcoin Cash (BCH) developers, designers & entrepreneurs. Network with other builders, win prizes & blaze a path into the BCH economy.

## Four steps to the fire

01

Register
### Sign Up

Register solo or as a team of up to four. Registration closes 13 November.

02

Skill Up
### Skill Up

Polish your skills with the info session and three live workshops packed full of BCH entrepreneurship & implementation advice from experienced industry mentors

03

Build
### Lock In & Build

Hack from the 14 Nov kick-off to 22nd Nov. Grab bounties and chase TokenHunt.

04

Showcase
### Showcase

Demo live at the Showcase on 28 Nov — winners and bounties announced.
