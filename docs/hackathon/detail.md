## QUESTION: I MISSED ROLL CALL?!! CAN I STILL COMPETE?!?!

ANSWER: Yes, but Rollcall has now closed, so there is a caveat! (This applies to both the Main & Media track)

If you are joining BLAZE after this point, you can still compete. You do not need to do roll call.

HOWEVER, because you did not complete roll call, your project will be in the low priority queue. Judges will look at it if they have time and only AFTER reviewing the projects who correctly completed roll call on time.

There are only 2 ways you can escape the low priority queue:

1.   Make a project so compelling that word of mouth or social media posts attract the attention of the judges. You may watch the Kickoff stream to hear the judges tips and advice on how to do this. [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWdo27QvzCo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWdo27QvzCo)

2.   Join an existing project that DID complete roll call as a new teammate. If you are able to convince someone to let you join their team, then great - but you will have to listen to their directions! Or if you're on a team already with a teammate who completed roll call, then likewise you are included in the high priority queue by default.

If you completed roll call, you will be in the high priority queue. Focus on making and promoting the best project you can.

Good luck everyone, happy building!

## Introduction

Welcome to BCH BLAZE 2026, the second edition of the BLAZE hackathon!!

BLAZE is a series of online hackathons designed to ignite innovation in the Bitcoin Cash (BCH) ecosystem! Hosted on DoraHacks, this event aims to attract new developers to Bitcoin Cash through a hands-on coding competition, give an opportunity for existing ecosystem players to show off their skills and empowering participants to build impactful prototypes. Whether you're a seasoned BCH developer or a curious coder from another community, join us to explore the scalable, low-fee Bitcoin Cash blockchain and create solutions that could shape the future of decentralized finance, payments, and beyond. Compete for a share of 25+ BCH in prizes, plus exclusive tickets to BLISS 2027 and other exciting perks!

## Judging

## Mainnet Track

Judges:

*   Elliot Price: Vice President at BCH Bull & founder of BCH Alliance
*   Kallisti: Lead developer of Selene Wallet
*   Sayoshi Nakamario, creator of FundMe.cash, Helpme.cash, Bitcoin Cash University & Badgers.cash

**3 judges, 4 categories scored out of 10 points, 120 points total, highest ranked projects win!!**

**1. Innovation & creativity (Is it cool?)**: Is the project new & creative? Does it make you excited to use BCH?

**2. Design & usability (Is it slick?)**: How easy is it to use this project? Is the UI intuitive? Does it look good?

**3. Technical strength (Does it work?)**: Does the project make good use of BCH’s monetary properties and/or scripting capability? Does it have bugs? Does it seem technically scalable to large quantities of users? Are there security or specification flaws?

**4. Business or Investment potential (Will it make money?)**: How likely is this project to turn into a profitable business? If you had funding to allocate to BCH projects, how likely & heavily would you be to allocate funding to this?

In the event of a tiebreak, judges pick their favourite between Project A & B, 2 out of 3 majority wins.

Purely up to Jeremy's judgement, so his favourite will win! But the key criteria is like any piece of media to be highly engaging & with potential to go viral, hopefully creating user education & interest in BCH.

The project with the most EMBER2026 tokens submitted to their project address wins. EMBER2026 tokens will be airdropped to BLISS Belle NFT holders.

## Note about Judge Timing

Judges have to review a large amount of submissions, and while time has been allocated for them to review things at more than a surface level, projects should not expect more than a couple of minutes of attention by default. Any extra attention will be earnt by having a compelling initial impression & Widget Factory Template explanation. All products should strive to be intuitive, fast, and get straight to user value - in BLAZE just like in the real marketplace. Unintuitive user experience or bugs that cause frustration & time-wasting will lose judge credibility and attention fast. Likewise with mockups or scaffold that aren't useable, judges will tune out and move on. Simpler things that work are better than complex things that are confusing or have rough edges. So be smart and don't rely on judges wading through a convoluted & complicated app - they will just mark it down and move on, as will potential end users!
